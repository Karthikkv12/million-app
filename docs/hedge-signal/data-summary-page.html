What this test can’t tell you
  • Options flow and dealer levels have no multi-year history, so they are shown on the desk as context only.
  • Option prices are modelled, not real fills. The IV model misses earnings premiums and ignores dividends and skew.
  • All symbols still trade today. The ETF and crashed-stock checks limit this survivorship bias but don’t remove it.
  • The 1.2× IV gate was chosen partly with hindsight. It only removes adds.
